## Prompt (final text)

Work in the $\mathbb{C}(q)$-linear pivotal category $\mathrm{Web}(\mathfrak{so}_{2n+1})$ of type $B$ webs, the diagrammatic category equivalent to the full subcategory of type I finite-dimensional $U_q(\mathfrak{so}_{2n+1})$-representations tensor-generated by the fundamental representations. Its generating objects are self-dual: vector-type strands labelled $1,2,\dots,n-1$, which quantize the exterior powers of the standard representation, and one spin strand $S$, which quantizes the spin representation. Strands labelled $n$ or higher do not exist. Morphisms are $\mathbb{C}(q)$-linear combinations of webs built from cups, caps and trivalent vertices. Fix $n=4$ and specialize $q=2$. For a nonzero scalar $v$ write $[k]_v=\frac{v^{k}-v^{-k}}{v-v^{-1}}$, and abbreviate $[k]=[k]_q$. Each of the four quantities below is a scalar in $\mathbb{C}(q)$; evaluate each at $q=2$ and reduce it to lowest terms.

Call a rung the endomorphism of a tensor power of $S$ given by the web in which a single $1$-labelled edge joins two neighbouring spin strands through one trivalent vertex on each of them, every other strand running vertically.

First quantity. On $S^{\otimes3}$ let $r_1$ be the rung joining the first and second spin strands and $r_2$ the rung joining the second and third. Let $w=q^{-1}r_1r_2+q\,r_2r_1$. The algebra of endomorphisms of $S^{\otimes3}$ is finite-dimensional over $\mathbb{C}(q)$, so there is a monic polynomial $\pi(t)$ of least degree with $\pi(w)=0$. The roots of $\pi$ are distinct, and each of them is a nonzero rational number when evaluated at $q=2$. The first quantity is the sum of those root values that are negative.

Second quantity. With $r_1$ and $r_2$ as above, there is a unique scalar $c\in\mathbb{C}(q)$ for which $r_1^{2}r_2+r_2r_1^{2}=c\,r_1r_2r_1+r_2$. The second quantity is $c$.

Third quantity. For certain indices $k$ there is a relation whose left-hand side is the web in which a $k$-labelled strand and a $1$-labelled strand rise into a horizontal spin edge whose two ends continue upward as spin strands, and whose right-hand side is a linear combination of two fork webs, in each of which the $k$- and $1$-labelled strands first merge into a single strand, labelled $k+1$ in the first fork and $k-1$ in the second, which then splits into two spin strands. The first fork carries coefficient $1$; the second carries a scalar coefficient depending on $n$ and $k$. Among the integers $k\ge1$ for which this relation is available at $n=4$, let $k_{-}$ be the smallest and $k_{+}$ the largest; they are different. The third quantity is the second coefficient at $k=k_{-}$ divided by the second coefficient at $k=k_{+}$.

Fourth quantity. Let $r$ be the rung on $S\otimes S$. Under the equivalence above, $S\otimes S$ corresponds to the tensor square of the spin representation, a $256$-dimensional $\mathbb{C}(q)$-vector space that decomposes as a direct sum of five pairwise non-isomorphic irreducible summands, of dimensions $126$, $84$, $36$, $9$ and $1$; the endomorphism by which $r$ acts on this space is scalar on each summand, and the five scalars are pairwise distinct. The fourth quantity is the ordinary linear-algebra trace of this endomorphism, that is, the sum of its $256$ eigenvalues counted with multiplicity, not the categorical trace obtained by closing the web, evaluated at $q=2$.

Formatting. Write each entry as a fraction in lowest terms in plain slash form $a/b$, writing a negative entry with a single leading minus sign as $-a/b$. Use no decimals, no LaTeX, no mixed numbers and no spaces anywhere. Report the four quantities as an ordered tuple, in the order in which they are introduced above, in the exact form $(a/b,c/d,e/f,g/h)$, output alone with no surrounding text, no $\boxed{\ }$ and no other formatting. ## Explanation (submission field)

- Conventions. All quantum numbers are evaluated at $q=2$: $[1]=1$, $[2]=5/2$, $[3]=21/4$, $[5]=341/16$, $[7]=5461/64$, $[9]=87381/256$. Writing $[2]_m=q^m+q^{-m}$: $[2]_2=17/4$, $[2]_5=1025/32$, $[2]_7=16385/128$. In the type $B$ web category the assignment $b_i\mapsto r_i$ (the rung joining spin strands $i$ and $i+1$) extends to a surjective algebra homomorphism $\psi$ from the $\iota$quantum group $U^{\iota}_{-q^2}(\mathfrak{so}_m)$ to $\mathrm{End}(S^{\otimes m})$, which factors through the quotient $U^{\iota}_{-q^2}(\mathfrak{so}_m)^{\le n}$ and induces an isomorphism onto $\mathrm{End}(S^{\otimes m})$. Set $p_{n+1}(t)=\prod_{j=1}^{n+1}\bigl(t-(-1)^{j-1}\tfrac{[2j-1]}{[2]}\bigr)$; here $n=4$, so $p_5$ has the five roots $\tfrac{[1]}{[2]},-\tfrac{[3]}{[2]},\tfrac{[5]}{[2]},-\tfrac{[7]}{[2]},\tfrac{[9]}{[2]}$.

In the finite quotient one has $p_{n+1}(b_{1,2})=0$, hence $p_5(w)=0$ on $S^{\otimes3}$, so $\pi(t)$ divides $p_5(t)$. For exactness: $b_{1,2}=\,^{\iota}T_1\,b_2\,^{\iota}T_1^{-1}$ with $^{\iota}T_1$ invertible, so $w$ is conjugate in $\mathrm{End}(S^{\otimes3})$ to $r_2=\mathrm{id}_S\otimes r$, where $r$ is the rung on $S\otimes S$.
