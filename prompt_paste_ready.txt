Let $(W,S)$ be a finite Coxeter system with length function $\ell$. For $J\subseteq S$ let $W_J$ denote the standard parabolic subgroup generated by $J$, let $X_J=\{w\in W:\ell(ws)>\ell(w)\ \text{for all}\ s\in J\}$ be the set of minimal length representatives of the left cosets of $W_J$ in $W$, and put $x_J=\sum_{w\in X_J}w$ in the integral group algebra $\mathbb{Z}W$. Solomon proved that the $\mathbb{Z}$-span of $\{x_J:J\subseteq S\}$ is a subalgebra of $\mathbb{Z}W$. For a field $F$ let $\mathscr{D}_F(W)$ denote the finite-dimensional $F$-algebra obtained from that subalgebra by extending scalars to $F$.

Recall that a finite-dimensional algebra has finite representation type if it has only finitely many indecomposable modules up to isomorphism, and that an algebra of infinite representation type is either tame or wild in the sense of Drozd, and never both.

Label Coxeter types so that the Coxeter diagram of a group of type $A_m$, of type $B_m$ and of type $D_m$ has exactly $m$ nodes in each case; so a Coxeter group of type $A_m$ is the symmetric group on $m+1$ letters, and one of type $B_m$ is the group of all signed permutations of $m$ letters.

Consider the twenty-four pairs $(W,p)$ in which $W$ is a finite Coxeter group of type $A_3$, $A_5$, $B_2$, $B_5$, $D_4$ or $D_6$, and $p$ is one of the primes $2$, $3$, $5$, $7$. For each such pair let $F$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p$, and let $s(W,p)$ denote the number of isomorphism classes of simple $\mathscr{D}_F(W)$-modules.

Let $f$ be the sum of the numbers $s(W,p)$ taken over exactly those pairs for which $\mathscr{D}_F(W)$ has finite representation type, let $t$ be the sum over exactly those pairs for which it has tame representation type, and let $w$ be the sum over exactly those pairs for which it has wild representation type.

Formatting. Each of $f$, $t$ and $w$ is a positive integer. Report them as an ordered triple in the exact form $(f,t,w)$, written as three bare integers separated by commas, with no signs, no decimals, no LaTeX, no spaces anywhere, and with no surrounding text, no $\boxed{\ }$ and no other formatting. What is that ordered triple?
